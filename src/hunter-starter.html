Hunter Starter Builds

Three PvE Hunter builds that are easy to play, for a newer player who already has One Thousand Voices with its catalyst. Each build uses One Thousand Voices as its heavy, so every other gun is a legendary you can farm. Nothing here needs a dungeon key.

Sandbox: Monument of Triumph (9.7.0) · Updated Nov 2026 · PvE only · No dungeon gear

Read this first

  • One Thousand Voices is your boss gun. Hold the trigger and it fires a beam on its own once it charges. The beam explodes where it lands, and the catalyst adds extra explosions. Aim at the boss's body, since crits don't matter.
  • Every build uses Gambler's Dodge. Dodging near enemies gives your melee back, so press dodge any time you're close to a fight.
  • Most of the guns come from the Tenet of Bravery in the Tower. Spend Legendary Marks on the engram whose loot pool has the gun you want. The pools are listed in each gun's note.
  • You don't need the exact roll. The two traits matter most. Barrel, magazine and masterwork are nice to have.
  • Mods and armor sets are my picks unless a build says otherwise. The guides only list a few of them.
Stats use the Edge of Fate system (Weapons, Health, Class, Grenade, Super, Melee). Every gun and perk was checked against Bungie's manifest (Nov 2026).
